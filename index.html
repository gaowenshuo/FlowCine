THE IDEA

Change the design.
Keep what makes it them.

Human-centric editing should change the world around a person without losing their identity or recorded performance. FlowCine brings a shared, training-free editing interface to video shots and native 3D human assets.

Natural-language instructions and independently optional scene, clothing, and object references define the desired appearance. Region-dependent control, residual correction, and detail transfer help preserve identity-related content as the visual design changes.

02 / METHOD

A small correction.
A shared principle.

Correct the generative flow toward the source where identity matters, while the target condition guides the edit.

01

Specify the appearance

An image editor combines a source view with the edit instruction and optional visual references to construct the target appearance.

02

Preserve identity locally

Head-focused residual correction preserves identity-bearing content while leaving clothing and surroundings free to follow the requested design.

03

Transfer source detail

Frequency transfer retains fine source information. The same principle extends to sparse 3D features through coordinate-aware filtering.

REGION-DEPENDENT CONTROL

Different regions.
Different constraints.

Mixed depth-and-edge control protects the head. Depth control follows the rest of the person; the background remains editable.

03 / MULTI-SHOT EDITING

One design.
Across the sequence.

Shared instructions and references coordinate a visual design across independently processed shots, adapting it to each pose and composition.

SHARED DESIGN

Alpine lakeside · Gingham shirt · Golden apples

The same supplied design is applied to different camera views and gestures. In split view, drag each divider to inspect the source and edited result.

04 / NATIVE 3D EDITING

The same person.
In another dimension.

FlowCine edits native shape and physically based appearance latents, producing reusable assets with modified geometry and materials.

A spatial head mask applies identity-focused correction across viewing directions. Coordinate-aware frequency transfer preserves detail on sparse 3D features.
